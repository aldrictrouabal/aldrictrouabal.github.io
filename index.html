Frequently asked questions

Working together

Can you work directly with our brand team?

From the brief to the roll-out, as an extension of your marketing and retail teams.

Do you work alongside agencies?

Design, 3D and images for the agency’s concept, in close step with its creative team.

How do you price?

Fixed fees by phase with named outputs, or a monthly retainer when I act as ongoing design leadership. For agency collaborations, a day rate.

Do you accept equity?

In selected cases with long-term potential, as a complement to fees.

Who else is on the team?

When a project calls for it, trusted specialists join in: graphic designer, UX strategist, motion designer.

Where are you based?

Nantes, working with clients worldwide.
